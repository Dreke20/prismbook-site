PrismBook Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026
Publisher: Dirty South Marketing Agency
Contact: dirtysouthmarketingagency@gmail.com

This policy describes how the PrismBook Android app (“PrismBook,” “we,” or “us”) handles information. PrismBook is an appointment booking app published by Dirty South Marketing Agency. Account, booking, and business data are stored in our Supabase (cloud) backend and, as a cache, on your device.

Information we collect

When you create an account or sign in, we collect:

  • Email address and password (your password is sent to our authentication service to create or verify your account; we do not display it in the app)

When you book, join a waitlist or walk-in queue, message a business, publish a review, or set up a storefront, we may also collect:

  • Name and phone number
  • Optional service address, if a business offers mobile or on-site visits
  • Intake notes, waiver/signature name, appointment details, and payment-at-visit preferences (for example “deposit due at visit”)
  • Messages and reviews you submit
  • Loyalty, membership, or gift-card balances a business records for you
  • For owners and staff: business profile, branding, services, staff roster, schedules, and payment records they enter

The current app does not ask you to enter a payment card number and does not process online card charges, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or NFC payments. If a business later records a card last-4 or in-person payment against a visit, that record is stored with the booking or customer profile.

The app also stores a local cache on your device, including your signed-in session tokens, so you can stay signed in and see a storefront offline.

How we use information

We use this information to provide the booking app: authentication, appointment scheduling, waitlists, messaging, reviews, business administration, and customer support. We do not sell your information and we do not use advertising or analytics SDKs in this build.

Where data is stored and who can see it

Account and app data are stored in our Supabase project and are protected with row-level access rules. A business you connect to can see the bookings, messages, and profile details associated with that business. We use HTTPS for network requests.

Permissions

  • Internet — to sign in and sync with the PrismBook backend
  • Notifications — optional local reminders if you allow them (the app does not send SMS or WhatsApp messages)

Account deletion

You can delete your account in the app: open Account (or Sign In while signed in) and choose Delete Account. This permanently removes your authentication account and associated appointments, waitlist entries, messages, reviews, and profile data from the backend, and signs you out. You can also uninstall the app to remove the on-device cache. Uninstalling alone does not delete your cloud account.

You can also request deletion without the app. See prismbook.dirtysouthagency.com/delete-account.

Children

PrismBook is intended for adults (18+) who are booking or running a service business. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the product changes. The effective date above will be revised when that happens. Questions: dirtysouthmarketingagency@gmail.com.
