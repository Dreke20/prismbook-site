PrismBook Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026
Publisher: Dirty South Marketing Agency
Contact: dirtysouthmarketingagency@gmail.com

This policy describes how the PrismBook Android app (“PrismBook,” “we,” or “us”) handles information. PrismBook is a booking-app preview with sample businesses. It is published by Dirty South Marketing Agency.

Information the app stores on your device

PrismBook keeps its data in on-device app storage (Android SharedPreferences). This can include:

  • The sample businesses that ship with the app, plus any business profile, branding, services, staff, bookings, waitlist, walk-in, loyalty, or POS records you create or change while using it
  • Names, phone numbers, addresses, intake notes, and similar details you type into a booking or kiosk form
  • A local owner PIN used only to unlock the in-app business admin and SaaS screens
  • Optional cloud-connection fields (a project URL and whether an API key was entered) if you use the developer setting in admin

The first launch is pre-filled with fictional sample clients and appointments so you can try the product. Those records are not real customer accounts.

If Android backup is enabled on your device, this on-device data may be included in your device backup.

What we do not collect

By default, PrismBook does not transmit bookings, customer details, payment information, or the owner PIN off your device. We do not have a user sign-up or login. We do not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or crash-reporting services in this build, and we do not sell your information.

Payments, deposits, gift cards, memberships, and the in-app point-of-sale are simulated. The app does not process real card charges and does not receive your actual payment-card number.

Optional network use

The app has permission to use the internet. The only built-in network feature is an optional connectivity check in the business admin screens. If you paste a project URL and API key there, the app may send a request to that URL to see whether it can be reached. That check does not upload your bookings or customer records. If you never enter those credentials, this request is not made.

Reminder “test send” uses the Android notification system on your phone. It does not send SMS, WhatsApp, or email.

Permissions

  • Internet — for the optional connectivity check described above
  • Notifications — so the app can show local booking or reminder alerts if you allow them

Children

PrismBook is intended for adults (18+) who are previewing a business booking tool. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can clear the app’s local data or uninstall PrismBook to remove information stored on the device. You can deny notification permission; the app will still open.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the product changes. The effective date above will be revised when that happens. Questions: dirtysouthmarketingagency@gmail.com.
