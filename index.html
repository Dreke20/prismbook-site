PrismBook: Booking Preview

Preview a booking app with sample shops. Book, waitlist, wallet & a demo POS.

PrismBook is a preview of a white-label appointment booking app for salons, spas, fitness studios, and mobile service businesses. This first Play build is a working demo. It opens on a sample barbershop and includes three other sample businesses you can switch to in the app: Apex Barber & Grooming Co., Lumina Aesthetics & MedSpa, Pulse Core & Reformer Studio, and Veloce Mobile Exotic Detailing.

What you can try

  • Customer booking: choose a specialist, select or chain services, pick a time, and confirm a slot
  • A waitlist and one-tap rebooking for the sample visits already in the app
  • A loyalty wallet with demo points, membership credits, and a gift-card balance
  • A front-desk kiosk for self check-in and a live walk-in queue
  • Business admin tools: a schedule and walk-in board, a brand QR poster you can share, and a point-of-sale checkout screen
  • Optional home-screen widget and a local reminder test

This is a preview. Sample clients, appointments, reviews, and card details are stored only on your phone. There is no sign-up or login. Payments, deposits, “card on file,” gift cards, memberships, tips, and the in-app POS are simulated — tapping charge or Tap-to-Pay does not take real money. This build does not include Stripe, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or NFC payment support. Features labeled “AI” are simple on-device keyword matching, and reminder templates do not send real SMS, WhatsApp, or email.
